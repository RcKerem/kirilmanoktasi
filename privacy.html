Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What we access

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through Login Kit, and the permission to upload and post videos (video.upload, video.publish).

How we use it

Only to publish our own approved clips to the connected account and to show the posting status inside the tool. We do not read followers, messages, or any other personal data.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally, encrypted, on the team's own computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to third parties other than TikTok's own API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected, or on request. You can revoke access at any time from TikTok › Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not used by them.
